ABOUT THE CEP PROJECT

Empowering small businesses with digital skills.

DigiGrow is a Community Engagement Project focused on practical digital marketing training for small business owners.

ABOUT DIGIGROW

Making digital marketing simple and practical.

DigiGrow is designed to help small business owners understand and use digital tools in their everyday business activities. The project provides simple explanations, practical examples and step-by-step training so that beginners can learn without needing advanced technical knowledge.

The training focuses on areas such as social media marketing, Canva, Google Business Profile, e-commerce and digital payments. Each topic is introduced in a simple way and supported with practical activities that learners can follow during the training.

OUR APPROACH

Learn skills you can apply.

DigiGrow follows a practical learning approach. Instead of focusing only on theory, the training encourages participants to explore digital platforms and apply what they learn to real business situations.

Participants can learn how to create a professional social media presence, design promotional materials, improve their visibility on Google, understand online selling and manage digital payments safely.

The aim is to help small businesses become more confident when using digital tools to promote their products and services, communicate with customers and build their online presence.

TRAINING AREAS

What you will learn.

Social Media Marketing: Learn how to create and manage a professional Instagram presence, publish posts and stories, create reels, engage with customers and understand basic account performance.

Canva for Small Business: Learn how to create posters, social media graphics, promotional offers and other simple business materials using Canva.

Google Business Profile: Understand how to create or claim a business profile, add business information, manage photos and reviews and understand basic business performance.

E-Commerce: Learn the basics of online selling, setting up an online store, adding products, managing orders, handling delivery and promoting an online store.

Digital Payments: Understand digital payment methods, UPI for business, transaction records, payment safety and how to handle common payment issues.

Digital Marketing Resources: Learn how to identify target customers, set marketing goals, choose suitable digital channels, create a content plan, promote a business and track results.

PROJECT OBJECTIVE

Learn. Apply. Grow.

The main objective of DigiGrow is to provide small business owners with accessible and practical digital marketing knowledge. By completing the training activities, participants can develop basic digital skills that can be applied to their own businesses.

The project also uses pre-training and post-training surveys to understand participants' learning experience and measure changes in areas such as digital marketing confidence and online presence.

DIGIGROW

Practical digital skills for small businesses.

DigiGrow brings together beginner-friendly explanations, practical activities, training resources and simple digital marketing guidance in one platform. The project is designed to make the first steps towards digital adoption easier for small business owners.
